## Features
-  Free, unlimited, interruption-free meditation sessions.
-  Flexible meditation categories to fit your mood.
-  No account creation required. No tracking. Your data stays on your device.
-  Works fully offline: all recordings stored on your device.
-  No ads, no subscriptions, and no paywalls. Just peace.

## User Journey
-  Homescreen displays a randomly chosen motivational quote to inspire the user, and a list of meditation types for the user to choose from.
-  Once the user chooses a mediation type, they are taken to a screen where there are multiple recordings of that type to choose to listen to.
-  Once the user selects a recording, they are taken to a player screen and can relax in the meditation audio.
-  They can play/pause, and skip forward and backward in the audio.

## Installation
You can install and run Zentra on your iPhone using the Xcode App Simulator.

### Requirements
- Mac w/ Xcode Version 15+
- An iPhone w/ iOS 17+

### Steps

1. Clone & Open Repository
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/ericysun/zentra
   cd zentra
   open Zentra.xcodeproj
2. Connect Your iPhone
- Plug your device into your Mac with a USB cable
- Or enable wireless debugging in Xcode → Devices and Simulators
- In the top toolbar, click the device dropdown and choose your iPhone.
- Press ⌘R to build and run the app on your device.
- Choose "Trust" when your iPhone asks you to trust the developer certificate.
